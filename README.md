### 🎯 Phase 1 Focus: Ecosystem Intelligence Dashboard

Our first product delivers actionable capital flow insights to institutional investors, government bodies, and enterprise innovation teams within **3 months of launch**:

- **Capital Flow Heatmaps**: Real-time visualization of funding by sector, stage, geography
- **Gap Analysis Engine**: Identify underfunded sectors despite strong technical signal
- **Predictive Signal Detection**: Leading indicators (papers, patents, hiring) that predict funding activity
- **Competitor & Portfolio Tracker**: For VCs — overlap detection, valuation benchmarks, exit watch
- **Policy & Grant Alert Engine**: Real-time monitoring of 500+ funding programs across UK/EU/US/Asia
